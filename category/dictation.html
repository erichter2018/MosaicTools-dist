Dictation & Speech

The custom speech-to-text ensemble: multiple engines voting on every word, with health monitoring, keyterm boosting, and instant paste.

🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 5.1

Ambient Voice Filtering (Speaker Gate)

EXPERIMENTAL — a one-time voiceprint of your voice lets the ensemble drop words spoken by other people (background conversation, TV) instead of dictating them into your report.
🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 3.7

Custom STT Mode

Replaces Mosaic's built-in speech recognition with MosaicTools' own multi-engine dictation pipeline — faster, more accurate on radiology vocabulary, and entirely under your control.
🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 5.0

Dictation Data Sharing (Opt-in)

Strictly opt-in sharing of de-identified dictation data to improve recognition accuracy — all PHI is stripped on your own machine before anything leaves it.
🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 3.7

Dictation Indicator & Beeps

A small floating window shows your words live as you dictate, and start/stop beeps confirm the recorder state without looking away from the images.
🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 5.2

Ensemble Health Light

A green/yellow/red dot on the main bar shows your speech engines' health at a glance; click it for a per-engine drawer with a plain-language reason when something is wrong.
🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 5.2

Failover Backup Engines

Spare engines you've set up but don't run in your ensemble automatically swap in when a member fails mid-shift — dictation keeps its full strength, then hands back when the engine recovers.
🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 5.0

Fix Transcript

A mappable action that presses Mosaic's transcript-cleanup button (the wrench icon) for you — no mousing to the Transcript toolbar.
🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 4.1

Keyterms & Auto-Learning

Radiology vocabulary sent to the speech engines to bias recognition toward the right words — plus auto-learning that harvests new terms from your own signed reports.
🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 4.1

Live Ensemble Metrics

A draggable always-on-top overlay showing what the ensemble is doing in real time — confidence, per-engine corrections and confirmations, and the measured accuracy impact.
🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 3.1

Mic Gain Calibrator

A built-in tool that listens to you dictate and recommends the right Windows microphone gain — so the speech engines get clean audio, neither too quiet nor clipping.
🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 3.7

Punctuation & Formatting

Controls how dictation becomes report text — auto vs. spoken punctuation, new-line behavior, radiology notation cleanup, and your own word replacements.
🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 2.1

Push-to-Talk (Dead Man's Switch)

Hold the Record button to dictate, release to stop — recording can never be left running by accident.
🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 5.2

Quick Setup Wizard

A guided, one-step-at-a-time wizard that creates the speech-engine accounts, captures and validates the API keys, and assembles the recommended ensemble for you.
🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 3.7

Speech Engines (Providers)

The roster of speech engines MosaicTools can run — all cloud engines are paid on your own account; medASR is free and runs locally on your workstation.
🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 4.1

STT Ensemble

Runs several speech engines on the same audio at once and merges them by word-level voting — engines correct each other, so the pasted text is better than any single engine alone.
🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 4.2

Transcript Comparison

A line-by-line view of the merged ensemble transcript with each engine's raw transcript underneath it — see exactly who heard what.
🎙️ Dictation & Speechsince 5.1

Turbo Paste

Near-instant pasting of dictated text that quietly refines itself in place as the full ensemble result arrives.
