Report Processing & QA

Process Report and the safety net around it — consistency checks, mismatch alerts, and sign-time guards.

🧾 Report Processing & QAsince 5.5.23

Auto-Remove Draft Phrases

Keep a checklist of exact statements the drafting AI keeps inserting that you never want, and MosaicTools deletes them from the draft automatically when the study opens.
🧾 Report Processing & QAsince 5.9.0

CT Dose

Reads CTDIvol and DLP off the scanner's own dose report and files them into TECHNIQUE and your transcript — the dose statement California sites are required to carry.
🧾 Report Processing & QAsince 4.2

Custom Process Report

An experimental alternate to Process Report where MosaicTools itself calls an LLM of your choice — Gemini, OpenAI, Groq, or xAI Grok, or all four racing in Quad Compare — instead of Mosaic's built-in one.
🧾 Report Processing & QAsince 5.6.0

Drafting Fixups

Optional, off-by-default corrections for the drafting AI's known bad habits — ETT position, central-line tip depth, and trailing zeros — applied to drafted text only, never your dictation.
🧾 Report Processing & QAsince 4.2

Findings/Impression Mismatch

Catches outright contradictions between FINDINGS and IMPRESSION — and, since 5.8.1, a FINDINGS section that contradicts itself — then pulses an alert until you fix or dismiss it.
🧾 Report Processing & QAsince 2.3

Gender Check

Flashing red alert when the report contains gender-specific terms that don't fit the patient — a prostate in a female patient's report, a uterus in a male's.
🧾 Report Processing & QAsince 4.2

Measurement & Laterality Consistency

Flags a pulsing red alert when a measurement or a left/right differs between FINDINGS and IMPRESSION, or when the report's laterality contradicts the study description.
🧾 Report Processing & QAsince 5.5.23

Native Process / Sign Watcher

Runs MosaicTools' usual post-Process/Sign follow-up even when you Process or Sign natively in Mosaic instead of through a MosaicTools button or hotkey.
🧾 Report Processing & QAsince 3.9

Patient Mismatch Alert

Pulsing yellow alert when InteleViewer is showing a different patient than the one open in Mosaic — the classic setup for dictating one patient's images into another's report.
🧾 Report Processing & QAsince 2.1

Process Report

The one-button core of the workflow — sends Alt+P to Mosaic to structure your dictation into the report, wrapped with auto-stop dictation, smart scrolling, and every post-process surface MosaicTools offers.
🧾 Report Processing & QAsince 2.1

Template Mismatch & Auto-Correct

A red-border alert when the loaded report template doesn't match the study type — with an optional attempt to fix it automatically by re-typing the study description into Mosaic.
🧾 Report Processing & QAsince 5.1

Unprocessed-Sign Warning

Beeps and shows a red warning box when you press Sign while dictation is still sitting unprocessed in the transcript box — press Sign again to sign anyway, or Process Report to capture it first.
