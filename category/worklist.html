Worklist & Productivity

TBWU pace tracking, critical-findings workflow, macros, and pick lists — the shift-management toolkit.

📈 Worklist & Productivitysince 3.5

Aidoc Alerts

Watches the Aidoc widget and raises an orange alert when Aidoc has a positive finding that's actually relevant to the study you have open — irrelevant flags are filtered out.
📈 Worklist & Productivitysince 5.1

Auto-Process on Open

Runs Process Report automatically the moment a drafted or capture study opens, so AI-drafted findings land in the Final Report before you've touched anything.
📈 Worklist & Productivitysince 2.1

Critical Findings

One button press scrapes the Clario exam note, formats the critical-communication details (who, when), and pastes a ready-made documentation line into your Mosaic report.
📈 Worklist & Productivitysince 2.7

Critical Notes

Create a Clario communication note without leaving Mosaic — including a one-press "Create Negative Critical" that documents a negative stroke read using Clario's canned message.
📈 Worklist & Productivitysince 2.7

Critical Studies Tracker

A session-long list of every study you placed a critical note on, behind a red ⚠ count on the bar — double-click any entry to reopen that study in Clario.
📈 Worklist & Productivitysince 3.9

Impression Fixer

One-click buttons that insert or replace impression text — your standard closers and disclaimers applied from the report popup (or directly inside the Mosaic editor) without dictating them.
📈 Worklist & Productivitysince 2.4

Macros

Auto-inserts your text snippets into the transcript when a study matching your criteria opens — with per-macro study-description rules, a drafted-only option, and optional auto-processing afterwards.
📈 Worklist & Productivitysince 2.6

Pick Lists

Structured text insertion from a popup — navigate a hierarchical tree or build sentences category-by-category, and paste the result straight into the report.
📈 Worklist & Productivitysince 2.7

Stroke Detection

Flags stroke studies with a purple alert the moment they open, and can create the Clario communication note for you — on a click, or automatically when you press Process Report.
📈 Worklist & Productivitysince 2.5

TBWU Counter

Live TBWU productivity metrics on the widget bar — total, per-hour, pace, and goal tracking — pulled from your local RADCounter database, with optional injection of live TBWU values into the Clario worklist.
