Measurement & Imaging

Auto-measure lesions, capture series/image numbers, and drive InteleViewer without touching it.

📏 Measurement & Imagingsince 4.3

Auto Measure

Point at a lesion in InteleViewer, press a button, and get an automatic bidimensional measurement drawn over the image — ready to paste into the report.
📏 Measurement & Imagingsince 2.1

Capture Series/Image

OCRs the InteleViewer viewport header to grab the current series and image numbers and inserts them into your report, formatted your way.
📏 Measurement & Imagingsince 2.7

Cycle Window/Level

One button steps InteleViewer through your window/level presets in order — lung, bone, soft tissue, brain — without reaching for the keyboard.
📏 Measurement & Imagingsince 4.3

Draggable Ruler Overlay

Puts a draggable, calibrated copy of InteleViewer's ruler on screen — drag it onto anything to measure, release and it snaps back home.
📏 Measurement & Imagingsince 5.7

Enable True Size Zoom (InteleViewer setup)

One-time InteleViewer setup that Spine Labeling and Auto Measure both need — calibrate your monitors' pixel pitch and add the True Size Zoom Factor to the CT image overlay, so MosaicTools can convert pixels to real centimeters.
📏 Measurement & Imagingsince 2.1

InteleViewer Buttons Toolbar

A floating, always-on-top toolbar of custom buttons that send your favorite InteleViewer shortcuts — designed in the built-in Button Studio.
📏 Measurement & Imagingsince 5.7

Spine Labeling

Get every vertebral body labeled in the sagittal viewport you are already looking at, then drag, add, delete, flip and renumber the labels before you accept — or press Quick Accept and take the best answer as-is.
📏 Measurement & Imagingsince 5.8

Visual Lesion Tracker

Mark the same lesion on several studies and get them side by side — captured at the same physical scale and measured by the same algorithm every time, so a size change is a real change and not measurement variation.
