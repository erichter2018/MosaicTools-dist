Measurement & Laterality Consistency

Flags a pulsing red alert when a measurement or a left/right differs between FINDINGS and IMPRESSION, or when the report's laterality contradicts the study description.

What it does

Three checks run automatically on the drafted report and surface as a pulsing red alert on the notification box:

  1. Measurement consistency — the same finding carries different sizes in FINDINGS vs IMPRESSION (a 4 mm calculus that became 5 mm in the impression).
  2. Laterality consistency — a structure is left in FINDINGS and right in IMPRESSION (or vice versa).
  3. Study description laterality — the report says "right metacarpal" but the study is a LEFT HAND. Suppressed for studies with bilateral components (AP pelvis, chest, etc.), and spine levels like L4/L5 are never mistaken for laterality.

How to turn it on / set it up

Settings → Alerts → Alert Triggers → Measurement & laterality consistency. The notification box itself must be enabled (Enable Notification Box, same tab) — the checker's alerts appear even in alerts-only mode.

Using it

You'll usually see it right after Process Report, once both sections exist. The alert names the discrepancy ("calculus: 4mm→5mm", "kidney: left→right"), and when the CDP connection is active the mismatched text flashes in the report editor itself so you can find it without hunting. Click the alert to dismiss it; dismissals are tracked per-mismatch and cleared when you change studies, so fixing one issue won't re-fire it but a new one still will.

Tips & gotchas

  • Measurement matching is one-to-one, so two findings that share anatomy keywords (two renal cysts of different sizes) don't cross-trigger false positives.
  • It compares FINDINGS against IMPRESSION — it can't know which of the two numbers is the correct one; that's your call.
  • This is a different check from the Findings/Impression mismatch alert, which catches contradictions of assertion ("no obstruction" vs "obstruction") rather than numbers and sides.
