Findings/Impression Mismatch

Catches outright contradictions between FINDINGS and IMPRESSION — and, since 5.8.1, a FINDINGS section that contradicts itself — then pulses an alert until you fix or dismiss it.

What it does

After both sections exist, MosaicTools compares FINDINGS and IMPRESSION for contradictions of assertion: one section negates a concept the other asserts. The classic case is a normal-template findings line ("No evidence of bowel obstruction") surviving next to a dictated impression point ("Small bowel obstruction") — usually the residue of an edited template or a processing hiccup. When it finds one, the notification box pulses (the settings label marks it pulsing orange) and names the contradiction.

Rewritten in 5.8.1. The check previously compared the two sections only against each other, so it was blind to a FINDINGS section contradicting itself — and in practice it only fired when a report was dictated non-literally. It now also catches:

  • Stale template negation — a leftover templated "no bowel obstruction" sitting in the findings while the same section (or the impression) describes the obstruction you actually dictated. This is the common real-world case: the template line was never removed.
  • Impression declares normal — an impression that calls the study normal or unremarkable when the findings describe something that isn't.

It's deliberately quiet: across a 6,078-report corpus it fires on 28 reports (0.46%), so an alert is worth reading rather than reflex-dismissing.

How to turn it on / set it up

Settings → Alerts → Alert Triggers → Findings/Impression mismatch. It requires the notification box (Enable Notification Box, same tab) and appears even in alerts-only mode.

Using it

The check runs automatically as the report is scraped — in practice you notice it right after Process Report. It matches medical concepts through synonym patterns plus proximity matching (all the significant words of a term in the same sentence), so "SBO" phrasings and reworded equivalents are still caught. With CDP active, the contradicting text flashes in orange in the report editor itself until corrected. Click the alert to dismiss; it clears on study change.

Tips & gotchas

  • Direction matters and is reported: findings-negates/impression-asserts is the common one, but the reverse fires too.
  • It's a concept-level check, not a grammar check — it won't catch a contradiction phrased in terms it has no concept for. Treat it as a net, not a guarantee.
  • Number and side discrepancies are the job of the Measurement & laterality consistency checker; run both.
