MosaicTools

The radiologist's companion for Mosaic, Clario, and InteleViewer

MosaicTools is a lightweight desktop companion that supercharges the Mosaic Reporting workflow — a multi-engine dictation ensemble, guideline-cited follow-up recommendations, report QA checks, prior summarization, productivity tracking, and dozens of quality-of-life upgrades. One small exe, no installer, auto-updating.
