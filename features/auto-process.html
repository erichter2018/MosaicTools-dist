Auto-Process on Open

Runs Process Report automatically the moment a drafted or capture study opens, so AI-drafted findings land in the Final Report before you've touched anything.

What it does

For hands-off study types, MosaicTools presses Process Report for you at study open:

  • Auto-process drafted studies on open — when a study arrives with an AI preliminary draft, Process Report runs immediately so the drafted findings are placed into the Final Report with no manual step (and drafts that arrive with findings still unprocessed get fixed).
  • Auto-process capture studies on open — same idea for CAPTURE studies (e.g. ultrasound tech-worksheet OCR), independent of the drafted toggle.
  • Auto-process all studies after macro insertion — runs Process Report once on every new study, but only after all of the study's opening macros have landed in the transcript. For workflows that insert a template macro at open and want it processed straight away.

How to turn it on / set it up

The drafted/capture toggles live in Settings → Drafting → Auto-process on open (moved from Behavior in 5.5.26); the after-macros toggle stays in Settings → Behavior. Enable whichever match your workflow; they're independent of each other.

Related: Ignore Inpatient Drafted (select all after auto-insertions) in the Inpatient XR Handling section auto-selects all text on inpatient X-rays after macro/clinical-history insertions, with a choice of All Inpatient XR or Inpatient Chest XR only — useful when you re-dictate those from scratch.

Using it

Open a drafted study; by the time you've looked at the images the findings are already in the Final Report and the impression is ready for your attention. It fires once per study open.

Tips & gotchas

  • Auto-process is skipped while you're actively dictating — it will not fire on studies you're talking into, only genuinely hands-off ones. It also skips addendums (macro-triggered variant).
  • It also skips a study whose entire draft is just "COMPARISON: None available." (since 5.5.23) — there's nothing to process, so it leaves the study alone (and still fires later if you dictate real content).
  • The macro variant waits for all matching opening macros before processing, whether a study matches zero, one, or several.
  • Combine with Auto-Stop/Auto-Restart Dictation and the sign warning for a fully guarded flow.
