Consult

One press shows the Matrix radiologists who are online in InteleViewer's chat - the right specialist for the study on screen first, general radiologists after - and a second press opens the chat with the one you pick.

IR/Vascular for contrast CT 6.2.6

Every contrast-enhanced CT ("with contrast", "without and with", "W/ contrast", CTA, runoff) now also offers an IR/Vascular section, after the study's own specialists. A CT without contrast does not.

What it does

Consult reads who is online in InteleViewer's chat directory (the Matrix_Rads group) and shows one button per radiologist. The study on screen decides the order:

Study Offered first
Head, face, neck, spine Neuro (spine also MSK)
Bones and joints of the limbs, shoulder, bony pelvis MSK
Abdomen and pelvis Body
Chest Cardiothoracic, then Body
Vessels (CTA outside the head, duplex, venous, runoff) IR / Vascular
Any MR MRI (after the region's specialist)
PET and nuclear medicine PET / NM
A patient under 18 Peds, before everything else

General radiologists always follow. You only see radiologists of your own section, and never yourself.

How to turn it on

Map Consult to a hotkey or a floating toolbar button in Settings → Keys & Buttons. InteleViewer must be open.

Using it

  • From a hotkey, a small window pops up by the mouse. From a floating button, it slides out from the button.
  • Click a name: MosaicTools opens InteleViewer's chat with that radiologist (about 2 seconds) and puts InteleViewer's people list away again. You type the message yourself.
  • Esc or clicking elsewhere closes the window.

Tips & gotchas

  • A name with * is online but not on the consult list, so it is shown as General.
  • Away and available look the same here - InteleViewer does not expose that status.
  • After InteleViewer restarts, the first Consult opens its people list once to load it.
  • The specialist list is built into MosaicTools; tell the developer when it changes.
