Auto-Remove Draft Phrases (now Text Rules) legacy / deprecated

Folded into Text Rules in 6.0.20. A rule with its replacement left blank deletes what it finds, which is exactly what this list used to do.

This is now part of Text Rules 6.0.20

The separate Auto-remove draft phrases list no longer exists. It — and Find & replace in drafts, and the word-replacement table that used to live under Text & Templates — were folded into a single Text Rules list in 6.0.20.

Your entries were moved across automatically the first time you ran 6.0.20. Nothing to re-enter.

A rule that leaves the replacement blank deletes what it finds, which is exactly what this list did. The match modes you had — exactly as written and any sentence containing all of these words — came across unchanged, and each rule now also says whether it applies to drafted text, to your own dictation, or to both.

The master switch came with it: Apply rules to drafted text, now at the top of the Text Rules editor rather than on the Drafting page. It is still off by default, because these remain the only rules that delete or rewrite draft text on their own.

Where it went

Settings → Drafting → Text rules, or Settings → Text & Templates → Text rules — both buttons open the same editor and the same list.
