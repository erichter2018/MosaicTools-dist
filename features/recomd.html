RecoMD Integration legacy / deprecated

Removed in 6.0.3 — the RecoMD service was discontinued. MTRec is the built-in replacement.

Removed in 6.0.3

RecoMD has been discontinued, so support for it has been taken out of MosaicTools entirely. The RecoMD integration and Auto-send on Process Report settings are gone, and the two mappable actions — Trigger RecoMD and Paste RecoMD — no longer exist.

If you had either action on a microphone button or a hotkey, that binding is released automatically when you update; the button is simply free again, and you can map anything you like to it. Nothing else about Process Report changes.

What replaces it

MTRec is the in-house engine and has been the recommended path since 5.3.0: offline, instant, no external agent and no window-scraping, with every recommendation drawn from verified guideline tables and closed with its citation.

Turn it on under Settings → MTRec, then either press the MTRec Recommendations key or let the MTRec boxes appear automatically after Process Report.
