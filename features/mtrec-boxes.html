MTRec Boxes

After Process Report, small clickable boxes appear next to "Final Report" — green "+" boxes insert a guideline recommendation into the impression with one click, amber "⚠" boxes are association reminders.

What it does

Instead of pressing the MTRec key and having recommendations merge immediately, the boxes give you a look-then-click flow. After you Process Report, once the impression has generated, MTRec runs automatically and posts its results as small boxes next to "Final Report" (e.g. "+ renal"):

  • Green boxes are available recommendations. Click one and it's inserted into the impression; nothing is inserted until you click, and Ctrl+Z undoes an insert.
  • Amber boxes are association reminders (only if MTRec Associations is enabled). Hover to see which companion findings the report hasn't addressed; click to open the full checklist modal.

How to turn it on / set it up

Settings → MTRec → "Show MTRec boxes after Process Report" — on by default once the MTRec engine itself is enabled. The manual MTRec Recommendations key keeps working exactly as before and is unaffected by this toggle.

Using it

Process the report, glance right of "Final Report". No boxes = MTRec found nothing actionable. A green "+ thyroid" means a guideline follow-up is one click away; click it, check the wording in the impression, move on. An amber "⚠ cirrhosis" is a nudge, not text — hover for the missing companions or click for the checklist.

Tips & gotchas

  • The boxes wait for the impression to generate — on a slow processing pass they appear a beat after the report does.
  • Since 5.5.3 the boxes anchor correctly in the stacked (top/bottom) layout too, not just side-by-side.
  • Box labels are displayed all-lowercase (as are the tooltips and checklist) — that's cosmetic, since 5.5.0.
  • Clicking green inserts exactly what the manual MTRec key would have inserted for that finding, citation included.
