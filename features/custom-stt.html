Custom STT Mode

Replaces Mosaic's built-in speech recognition with MosaicTools' own multi-engine dictation pipeline — faster, more accurate on radiology vocabulary, and entirely under your control.

What it does

Custom STT Mode takes over dictation from Mosaic's built-in speech recognition. Your microphone audio goes to one or (usually) several speech engines in parallel, the results are merged, run through radiology-aware text processing (units, spine levels, punctuation, your own word replacements), and pasted into the Mosaic report. You keep your normal workflow — Record button, transcript, Process Report — but the recognition underneath is yours to choose and tune.

How to turn it on / set it up

  1. Open Settings → Speech-to-Text and check Enable Custom STT Mode.
  2. Click ⚡ Quick Setup Wizard — it walks you through creating the engine accounts and API keys step by step, then assembles the recommended ensemble for you.
  3. When Mosaic shows its WebHID device prompt, cancel it (MosaicTools handles the microphone). The wizard also helps turn off Mosaic's "Auto Transcript Record" so Mosaic doesn't grab the mic first.

Cost honesty: the cloud engines are paid, on your own account — typically fractions of a cent per minute of dictation, pay-per-use. The on-device medASR engine is free.

Using it

Dictate exactly as before. Text appears near-instantly (see Turbo Paste) and quietly refines as the other engines weigh in. A floating indicator shows live transcription, beeps confirm start/stop, and Auto-start on case open can begin recording automatically when a new study opens (stopping on Sign Report).

Tips & gotchas

  • Run the wizard rather than entering keys by hand — it validates each key with a real connection to the provider.
  • One engine works; the ensemble is where the accuracy gain lives.
  • False starts inside a sentence are now collapsed (5.6.1). When you abandon a phrase and restart it mid-sentence, the abandoned words are dropped before the paste: "…there are diffuse flowing os there are diffuse flowing osteophytes throughout…" pastes as just the completed phrase. This previously only worked when the two halves arrived in separate pastes — both halves landing in one paste went through uncorrected.
  • If dictation misbehaves, check the ensemble health light on the toolbar first — a dead engine (usually billing) is the most common cause.
