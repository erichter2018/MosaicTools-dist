MTRec: deterministic, guideline-cited follow-up recommendations and the Associations completeness checklist.

📋 Recommendationssince 5.4

MTRec Associations

A completeness checklist against satisfaction-of-search — for each major finding you dictate, a small popup lists the companion findings that usually travel with it, marked addressed, missing, or N/A for this study type. It never inserts text.
📋 Recommendationssince 5.5

MTRec Boxes

After Process Report, small clickable boxes appear next to "Final Report" — green "+" boxes insert a guideline recommendation into the impression with one click, amber "⚠" boxes are association reminders.
📋 Recommendationssince 5.3

MTRec Recommendations

The in-house, deterministic recommendation engine — detects incidental findings in your report and merges the matching guideline follow-up recommendation, with citation, into the impression on a single key.

RadAI Impression
