MosaicTools

The radiologist's companion for Mosaic, Clario, and InteleViewer

MosaicTools is a lightweight desktop companion that supercharges the Mosaic Reporting workflow — a multi-engine dictation ensemble, guideline-cited follow-up recommendations, report QA checks, prior summarization, productivity tracking, and dozens of quality-of-life upgrades. One small exe, no installer, auto-updating.

✨ New in v6.2.8

v6.2.8
  • NEW: Fix Transcript keeps your drafted text and the dashed line as they were - only your dictation is fixed.
  • NEW: Behavior setting "Fix Transcript before every Process Report" (off by default).
  • NEW: MosaicTools offers to move itself out of OneDrive / Program Files into its own folder, with a desktop
  • NEW: Ultrasound worksheets in the drafted box get body-part tags (gallbladder separate from liver).
  • NEW: Clario shows an hourly work-unit bar graph under the TBWU row (with RVUCounter 5.2.7+).
  • CHANGED: The anatomy/drafted box also shows on CT head (drafted labels; no anatomy model there).
  • CHANGED: Discard is faster in Mosaic 2.0.5.4. The dashed line lands sooner and always right under the draft.
  • CHANGED: A failed update now says why.
v6.2.7
  • NEW: Show anatomy "dots" view - a dot on each structure; rest the mouse on any part of one to see its name
  • CHANGED: MTRec writes an emphysema note at every age - over 80 "outside screening age", under 50 "unusual at
  • FIXED: With custom STT on, the PowerMic/SpeechMike Record button no longer also starts Mosaic's own dictation
v6.2.6
  • NEW: Consult adds an IR/Vascular section for every contrast-enhanced CT.
