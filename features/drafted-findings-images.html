Drafted Findings Within Images

Writes each drafted finding on the InteleViewer images while you scroll — in the black air beside the body, on the images where that organ usually is.

What it does

While you scroll an axial series in InteleViewer, each drafted finding appears as a small framed box beside the body, on the images where its organ most likely is. "Cholelithiasis" shows up with the gallbladder; the spine shows below the body; a bilateral finding sits in the middle.

The drafting AI gives no image numbers, so every place is an estimate from anatomy — which organ the sentence names, and where that organ usually sits in the stack. Works on CT abdomen/pelvis, CT and CTA chest, and plain head CT (where image 1 is the skull base).

How to turn it on / set it up

Settings → Drafting → Drafted Findings Popup → Show drafted findings within images. It is a child of the drafted findings popup, so that must be on too. Off by default.

Its child, Dim matched findings: off, a finding your dictation already covers is taken off the images; on, it stays, dimmed.

Using it

Click the axial series so it has the yellow frame, then scroll. Brightness and weight follow the popup — loud findings white and bold, quieter ones grey. Sentences the draft wrote on one line stay together in one stack, in their written order. A box keeps its place once shown; it only moves to get off the anatomy, and only outward. Zoom in or out and the boxes are re-placed for the new view.

Click a box to clear it, the same as clicking its row in the popup: the finding is removed from the transcript. Click a dimmed (matched) box to bring it back. Clicks anywhere else, and the mouse wheel over a box, still go to InteleViewer. (since 6.1.1)

In a wide window (W over 800 — lung, bone), blankets and the table light up and look like patient. The boxes stay where the soft-tissue window put them. (since 6.1.1)

Tips & gotchas

  • An estimate, not a detection: it tells you roughly where to look, not where the finding is.
  • Axial only. A combined study (chest/abdomen/pelvis, head plus face or spine) gets no labels rather than wrong ones.
  • Boxes avoid MosaicTools' own windows, InteleViewer's corner text and its R/L/A/P letters.
  • When there is no air left for a box, it goes where it covers the least patient.
