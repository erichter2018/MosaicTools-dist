Drafting Fixups

Optional, off-by-default corrections for the drafting AI's known bad habits — ETT position, central-line tip depth, and trailing zeros — applied to drafted text only, never your dictation.

What it does

A family of targeted, off-by-default corrections for things the drafting AI gets consistently wrong. Each one rewrites the drafted text in the transcript box (and the drafted-findings popup) once per study, the moment the draft loads — before you start editing. Your own dictation is never touched, and every fixup is deterministic (no AI rewriting AI).

Rewrite ETT position — the drafting AI reports a spurious "tip is X cm from the carina" measurement. This converts it to the tracheal-third description most reports actually want: "…tip is 4.2 cm from the carina" → "…tip is in the middle third of the trachea." Below-carina (endobronchial) phrasings are left alone — those need your eyes.

Rewrite central line tip position (new in 5.5.26) — the drafting AI consistently places a central-line tip one level deeper than it really is. This shifts a drafted tip position one level back up the SVC ladder: right atrium → cavoatrial junction → lower third → middle third → upper third of the SVC. The upper third is the top of the ladder and is never rewritten further. Pacemaker/ICD leads and Swan-Ganz catheters are never touched (their tips legitimately sit deep), and any sentence describing two positions at once is skipped.

Strip trailing zeros from drafted measurements (new in 5.5.26) — the drafting AI writes whole-number measurements with a trailing zero ("3.0 mm stone", "11.0 x 8.0 cm") on nearly one in five of its measurements. Trailing zeros are a classic misread hazard ("2.0" read as "20") and against AMA style. This drops the ".0" from whole numbers inside drafted measurements — numerically identical, just safer: "11.0 x 8.0 x 12.0 cm" → "11 x 8 x 12 cm", "1.0-2.0 mm" → "1-2 mm". Real decimals (2.5 cm) and leading zeros (0.2 cm) are untouched.

How to turn it on / set it up

Settings → Drafting → Drafting Fixups (experimental). Each fixup is its own checkbox, all off by default — enable only the ones whose failure mode you've seen. Requires CDP (the direct-DOM connection to Mosaic).

Using it

Nothing to do — open a drafted study and the enabled fixups run once, within a tick or two of the draft loading. The drafted-findings popup refreshes to show the corrected text, and Process Report carries the corrected transcript into the final report.

Tips & gotchas

  • These are position/format rewrites of AI-drafted text — always verify against the image. The central-line shift in particular encodes an observed bias (one-level-too-deep); if a draft happens to be right, the shift makes it wrong by one level in the other direction. That's why it's off by default and experimental.
  • The fixups run once per study on the drafted prelim. Text you dictate afterwards is never rewritten — dictating "two point zero" keeps the .0 you asked for.
  • The trailing-zero strip is the safe one of the family: it never changes a value, only its rendering.
