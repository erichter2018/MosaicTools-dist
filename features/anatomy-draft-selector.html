Anatomy / Draft Selector

A small box over your CT - Show anatomy labels what is on the slice (organs, bones, vessels, muscles), Show drafted puts the drafted findings on the real organ.

XR1 first, red count, fewer crossing lines 6.2.6

On an X-ray, Show anatomy now starts with XR1; click Anatomy again for FleXray. In Show drafted the count turns light red while drafted statements are left. On CT, when two label lines cross (or a line runs under another label), the two labels trade places, so far fewer lines cross.

Drafted count and XR1 v3 6.2.5

In Show drafted, the line under the buttons reads drafted statements: N - the drafted statements your dictation has not covered yet (comparison lines are not counted). XR1 v3 replaces v2: far fewer wrong labels on real films. Forearm, humerus, femur and tib-fib films now show the joints at each end, and humerus, radius, talus and calcaneus X-rays get the box again (their names were read as ultrasound).

What it does

A small box on screen with four buttons:

  • Show anatomy - labels what is on the axial slice you are looking at: organs, the five lung lobes, bones, vessels and muscles (68 structures), each with a thin line to a dot on it. Handy for anatomy you do not see every day.
  • Show drafted - the drafted findings on the images, as before, but now placed by the real anatomy on the slice (see below).
  • Show nothing - hides both.
  • Copy - puts the pane with its anatomy labels on the clipboard. The labels themselves are left out of ordinary screenshots on purpose; the box itself is not.

Right-click Show anatomy for a small drawer: All, Muscles, Bones, Vessels, Organs. Right-click Show drafted for Dim matched findings, a text size slider, and the frame/line color of the drafted labels (White, Cyan, Blue, Green, Pink, Red, or the default grey) - the same settings as on the Drafting page. Your choices are kept.

Show drafted cycles (since 6.2.3): each click steps through Drafted (the drafted findings), Mine (your own dictated sentences, placed the same way) and Both. Right-click it to tick Default view - the view each new study opens in - and to pick a separate colour for drafted labels and for yours. Copy on any of these views copies the pane with those labels.

Plain films work too: Show anatomy names the bones and organs on the film, with each label right beside its structure - on the film, since a radiograph has no air to keep to. Since 6.2.3 it shows only the bones that fit the study (a foot study shows no hand or thigh) and takes the side from the study name. Show drafted / Show nothing switch the drafted X-ray labels.

XR1 (since 6.2.3) is MosaicTools' own X-ray anatomy model. On a plain film the button reads Anatomy: FleXray; click it to switch to Anatomy: XR1 (downloads 48 MB once). It is an early version - tell Erik about any wrong label.

The anatomy model (A1)

A1 is MosaicTools' own model. It looks at the one slice on screen, as displayed, and outlines what it recognises. On scans it never saw it matched the reference outlines about 85% (Dice 0.85; 0.82 on scans from other hospitals). Big organs, lungs, bones and large muscles are the most reliable; small structures (adrenals, prostate, thin vessels) less so. It is not a medical device and does not look for disease - it only names anatomy and decides where a label goes.

Turning the setting on downloads A1 once (48 MB). It runs on the graphics card when there is one; Settings → Profile → Use the graphics card (GPU) for AI models (on by default, since 6.2.3) turns that off for every MosaicTools AI model if the graphics card causes trouble.

A1 was trained on two public datasets, both CC BY 4.0: the TotalSegmentator CT dataset (Wasserthal et al., Radiology: AI 2023) and AMOS 2022 (Ji et al., NeurIPS 2022).

What changes for the drafted findings

With the selector on, the drafted findings on the images use A1:

  • When: a finding about an organ shows on the images where A1 actually sees that organ (the image number is only a loose check against the impossible).
  • Where: a new label goes beside where the organ really is on that slice, with a thin line to it.
  • Chest/abdomen/pelvis CT now gets labels - whether it is one long stack or a separate chest series and abdomen/pelvis series. Where A1 stops seeing lung is the lung bases: above them the chest findings, below them the abdomen/pelvis findings.
  • Anything A1 does not outline (effusions, nodes, free fluid, the brain) keeps the old placement.

Vertebral levels

If the sagittal has spine labels (Spine Labeling), a drafted finding that names a level ("L4 compression fracture") shows exactly on that level's slices. With Show anatomy on, the vertebra label reads "L3 vertebral body" or "L3-L4 disc space", and the separate level box steps aside.

Tips & gotchas

  • Shows while a CT or CTA of the neck, chest, abdomen or pelvis, or a plain film, is open in Mosaic.
  • On X-rays the rib labels are hidden.
  • Drag the box by its title strip; it stays where you put it.
  • Labels keep their spot while you scroll; only the line moves.
  • Labels avoid MosaicTools' windows and InteleViewer's corner text, and use the air beside the body.
  • Not yet labelled (no public data): pelvic floor, pelvic and neck lymph node regions, pancreas head/body/tail, lung segments. Kidney cysts are hidden for now.
