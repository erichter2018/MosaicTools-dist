Dictation Indicator & Beeps

A small floating window shows your words live as you dictate, and start/stop beeps confirm the recorder state without looking away from the images.

What it does

Two kinds of feedback keep you confident the recorder heard you. The dictation indicator is a minimal dark floating strip that shows live transcription while you speak — interim words in gray, finalized words in white (or confidence-tinted when Text coloring is on, including magenta for a flagged non-word). It clears after each paste, lingers a couple of seconds when recording stops so you can verify the tail of what you said, then hides. The beeps are short tones on recording start and stop, so you know the mic state without ever looking at the screen.

How to turn it on / set it up

  • Indicator: Settings → Speech-to-Text → Display → Show dictation indicator.
  • Beeps: Settings → Speech-to-Text → Beeps → Start beep / Stop beep, each with its own volume slider.

Using it

Drag the indicator wherever it doesn't cover anatomy — it remembers its position, grows downward as you speak longer passages, and never steals focus from Mosaic. Most people park it near the report column and stop consciously noticing it, glancing only when a word sounds risky.

Tips & gotchas

  • The indicator shows the driver's live stream; the pasted report text can differ slightly after the ensemble's corrections land — the report is the source of truth.
  • Keep beeps on even if you drop the indicator: a missing stop beep is the fastest tell that a recording is still running.
  • There's also a System Beep action you can map to a button for audible dictation-state feedback when using Mosaic's built-in dictation.
